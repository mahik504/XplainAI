# XplainAI ? Product Specification

## 1. Product Summary
XplainAI is an Explainable Intelligence workspace that converts generative AI from an opaque text generator into a transparent, verifiable research platform.

## 2. Core Value Propositions
1. **Observable Verification**: Every key assertion in an AI response is mapped to specific evidence from primary sources.
2. **Contradiction & Assumption Exposure**: Highlights where sources disagree or where the AI is making ungrounded inferences.
3. **Interactive Investigation**: Users can click any statement in the text or graph to inspect raw source snippets and challenge assertions with one-click "Evidence Demand".
4. **Zero Pseudo-Reasoning**: Respects privacy and security by strictly presenting verified external evidence rather than hallucinated model internal thoughts.

## 3. User Workflows
1. **Research & Explore**:
   - User types an open research question.
   - Selects orchestration mode: **Fast** (direct verified answer), **Balanced** (standard multi-source search), or **Deep Research** (exhaustive multi-task investigation).
   - Observes live discovery stage cards and source intake.
2. **Inspect & Verify**:
   - Explores the synthesized answer with color-coded claim highlights.
   - Interacts with the synchronized 2D Flow or 3D Constellation graph.
   - Clicks a claim to highlight its evidentiary chain and view verified source excerpts.
3. **Challenge & Demand Evidence**:
   - Identifies an unverified assertion or contradiction.
   - Clicks `? Demand Evidence` to generate a targeted follow-up probe in the composer.\n
