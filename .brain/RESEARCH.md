# XplainAI ? Research Log & Technical Principles

## State of the Art in Explainable AI UX
1. **NotebookLM (Google)**: Proved the value of strict document grounding with highlighted inline citations. Users trust answers when clicking a citation scrolls directly to the verified primary passage.
2. **Perplexity Pro Search**: Demonstrated multi-step research execution with live step-by-step query decomposition and source badges.
3. **Elicit / Consensus**: Established structured evidence extraction tables comparing multiple claims across papers.
4. **Connected Papers & ResearchRabbit**: Proved that visual citation graphs provide instant intuitive grasp of how concepts and papers connect.

## Synthesis for XplainAI
XplainAI combines the best of all four paradigms into a unified, live intelligence workspace:
- **Live Discovery**: Step-by-step research stage tracking.
- **Source Grounding**: Verifiable snippet extraction and direct URL verification.
- **Claim-Evidence Graph**: Visual 2D/3D ontology linking claims to their exact supporting evidence.
- **Observable Rigor**: Contradiction detection, missing context warnings, and interactive evidence demand.\n
